Your lab, step by step

  1. Draw in MolDraw and export a MOL file. For a 3D model, use a 3D SDF or XYZ export.
  2. Choose the molecule and the correct model tab, then upload. Flat files may be valid for planar molecules, but a 2D drawing cannot establish 3D VSEPR geometry.
  3. Measure uploaded coordinates here. For VSEPR versus QM comparisons, use instructor-approved starting coordinates and the optimized output from WebMO.
  4. Run energies and optimizations in WebMO. Use consistent method, basis set, charge and multiplicity. Carbonate has charge −2; bicarbonate −1. Confirm the charges for intermediates with your protocol.
  5. Transfer geometry into the table; enter energy in kJ/mol. If WebMO reports hartree, multiply by 2625.49964. Do not mix energy methods.
  6. Save structure PNGs, add notes, and export your report. Print the report to PDF if needed. Save a JSON backup for all editable work.
